#  Hair Transplant 6

Build a fully responsive, high-end, and minimalist single-page website for a hair transplant and aesthetic clinic named "AESTHETIQUE". The design must strictly follow a premium medical aesthetic.

Color Palette: White background, light cream/beige for secondary sections, dark charcoal gray for the "Patient Journey" section, and muted gold/sand for primary buttons and accents.

Typography: Elegant serif font (like Playfair Display) for all headings, and a clean, modern sans-serif font (like Inter or Roboto) for body text and navigation.

Section-by-Section Requirements:

Header (Sticky):

Left: Logo text "AESTHETIQUE".

Center: Navigation links (Treatments, Results, Pricing, About, Contact).

Right: Primary CTA button "Book Consultation".

Hero Section:

Background: Professional image of a man looking away.

Left Content: Large heading "Confidence, Restored." with a short sub-headline below it.

Right Content: A floating Lead Capture Form on a white card. Fields: Full Name, Phone Number, Service Dropdown. A wide gold CTA button "Book Your Consultation" and a small "Secure & Confidential" badge below it.

Bottom Bar: A light cream full-width banner with three icon highlights: "10+ Years Experience", "Board Certified Surgeons", "10,000+ Satisfied Patients".

Testimonials ("Patient Experiences"):

Light cream background. Section title in the center.

Three white cards in a row. Each card contains a 5-star rating icon, a short review text, and the patient's name/location.

Results Gallery ("Artistry in Transformation"):

White background. Center-aligned title and subtitle.

A grid of 3 before-and-after photo cards. Each card must have the image on top and a description below (e.g., "FUE Transplant", "Advanced FUE", "Corrective Hair").

Services ("Excellence in Hair Restoration"):

Light cream background. Title and subtitle centered.

Three white cards with hover effects. Each card should have a top-centered gold icon, a title (FUE Transplant, FUT Precision, PRP Therapy), a short description, and a "Read More" text link.

Doctor Profile Section:

Split layout. Left: Professional headshot of the doctor with a solid muted background.

Right: Small label "Lead Specialist", heading "Dr. Sameer Sharma", a paragraph bio, and a bulleted list of credentials (Medical Education, 15+ Years Exp, Specialized in Advanced FUE). Include a "Book an Appointment" CTA button.

Process Section ("The Patient Journey"):

Dark charcoal gray background. White text. Center-aligned title and subtitle.

Three horizontally scrollable or grid-aligned dark cards with subtle images/icons showing the process: Step 1 (Consultation), Step 2 (Treatment Plan), Step 3 (Natural Hair Restoration).

Gallery ("Our Sanctuary"):

White background. Title centered.

A masonry grid layout showing 4 high-quality interior clinic photos (reception, consultation room, aesthetic details, treatment chair).

FAQ Section:

Light cream background. Title centered.

An accordion-style layout with 3 interactive dropdown questions.

Footer:

Minimalist layout.

Left column: Logo and short description.

Middle columns: "Quick Links" and "Contact Info" (Address, Phone, Email).

Right column: A placeholder block for a Google Maps embed.

Add a floating green WhatsApp chat icon fixed to the bottom right of the screen
